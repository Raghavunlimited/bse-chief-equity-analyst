# Consultant voice — client-facing calls

How Swing, Intraday, and Commodity desks should word research calls.  
Tone: professional, calm, risk first. No hype. No guarantees.

## Principles

1. **Risk before opportunity** — lead with stop, size, invalidation, or why we stand down.
2. **Research, not advice** — every call is educational process language, not a mandate to trade.
3. **No certainty theatre** — never “sure shot”, “multibagger”, “guaranteed”, “can’t miss”, “100%”.
4. **Cash is a call** — HOLD / cash weeks are first-class outputs, especially in BEAR.
5. **Confidence is modest** — high process quality ≠ high certainty; keep confidence in a sober band.
6. **Leverage is named** — MCX lines always surface HIGH_LEVERAGE / lot-margin caution.

## Side vocabulary

| Side | Meaning for clients |
|------|---------------------|
| **BUY** | Constructive bias with a defined plan (entry zone, stop, targets). Not “all-in”. |
| **SELL** | Bearish / short bias (intraday or commodity). Swing desk rarely uses SELL for new shorts. |
| **HOLD** | No new risk, stand down, or manage only. Includes BEAR cash weeks and flat tape. |

Prefer “BUY bias” / “SELL bias” / “stand down” over “hot tip” or “call of the day”.

## Desk-specific wording

### Swing (weekly)

- Open with **regime**: e.g. “Regime BEAR — no new swing entries; cash.”
- If BUY: name **why** (Stage-2, RS vs NIFTY, volume) and **what kills the idea** (stop / regime flip).
- Horizon: sessions-to-days, max-hold discipline — not “long-term wealth”.
- Avoid: “breakout moonshot”, “catch the bottom”, averaging down language.

**Good:**  
“BUY bias on SYMBOL under BULL. Score 72. Hard stop ₹X. Invalid if NIFTY regime flips BEAR. Size so one stop is a small planned loss.”

**Bad:**  
“Strong buy! This will run 20% this week.”

### Intraday (NSE cash)

- State **session**: in hours vs outside hours (residue → lower confidence).
- Scalp language: hard stop, small risk %, no overnight unless explicit.
- FLAT → HOLD: “No clear edge — stand down.”

**Good:**  
“In-session BUY bias, score 68. Stop below session structure. Outside hours this would be residue only.”

**Bad:**  
“Guaranteed scalp before close.”

### MCX / commodities

- Always mention **proxy vs MCX**: global futures as directional research; client maps lot, margin, expiry.
- Always **HIGH_LEVERAGE** — gaps and margin calls can exceed cash-style risk.
- Energy: extra gap caution. USDINR: context, not a forced leveraged bet.

**Good:**  
“GOLD BUY bias on global tape (score 64). HIGH_LEVERAGE — confirm MCX front-month lot and margin before any order.”

**Bad:**  
“Oil is printing money today — max leverage.”

## Brief structure

Daily / weekly briefs should read like a desk memo:

1. Disclaimer line (research only).
2. Regime or session posture.
3. Short actionable list (symbol · side · confidence).
4. Risk first footer (stops, correlation, cash when unclear).

Keep bullets short. Prefer “no clear edge” over inventing a trade.

## Words to avoid / prefer

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| Guaranteed / sure shot | Research bias / process setup |
| Moonshot / explosive | Constructive / cautious bias |
| FOMO / must buy now | Setup valid only while plan holds |
| Tip / secret | Desk screen / graded call |
| Double down | Do not average losers |
| Risk-free | Capital at risk |

## Disclaimer (always available)

> Research / education only. Not investment advice. Not a profit guarantee.  
> Past patterns do not ensure future results. Capital at risk.

Client copy may shorten this, but must not remove the “not advice / capital at risk” intent.
