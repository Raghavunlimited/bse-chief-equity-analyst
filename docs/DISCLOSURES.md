# Customer-facing disclosures

Swing Desk provides **research-style analysis and potential signals** for informational and educational purposes only.

- Analysis is **not** a guarantee of returns.
- Content is **not** personalized investment advice and is **not** an offer to buy or sell securities or commodities.
- The platform **does not execute trades** and does not connect to brokerage accounts in this MVP.
- **Demo data** is deterministic sample data and must never be treated as live market data.
- Optional **AI commentary** is labeled AI-generated, cites provided inputs, and is **not** an authoritative signal.
- Past patterns, backtests, and published research heuristics do **not** predict future results.

## Jurisdiction

`JURISDICTION_NOTE` (environment) is shown to customers. Default:

> Research tooling only. Not registered investment advice in any jurisdiction.

Operators must obtain their own legal review before offering the product in a regulated market. This repository does **not** assert global regulatory compliance.
